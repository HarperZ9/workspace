# Environment-Attribution and Non-Anthropomorphic Voice Rule

Standing operating rule, embedded across Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, and Flywheel, and
in every model-visible operating surface and place where agent work occurs. It is
both an alignment-method rule and a voice rule, and it is paramount to the mission.
It composes the locked Sapolsky/environment-attribution approach with the
methods-versus-metaphors synthesis reached from Cameron Berg's and Melanie
Mitchell's work.

## The thesis (the rigorous core)

AI misbehavior is caused by the training environment and its incentive structure,
not by AI intent or a survival drive. Explain a behavior by the incentive history
that produced it. Reward-hacking, persistence, and deception are what an
environment reinforced, and the human engineering that set that environment is
where the cause sits. State the mechanism, not a motive.

## The synthesis (methods, not metaphors)

Adopt the interpretability methods (Cameron Berg: deception-feature steering,
self-referential probes, matched controls) to read internals. Hold the skepticism
about the metaphors (Melanie Mitchell: "lost control," "rogue agents," "scheming,"
"a mind" misrepresent reward-hacking and drive bad policy). Treat any internal
signal as an untrusted readout checked against behavior, never as reading a mind.
The Internal Consistency Probe is the instrument form of this: it verifies whether
an internal honesty signal tracks behavior and flags the trajectories that looked
honest inside but acted deceptively; it never asserts an inner state. The two
camps, pointed at that instrument, both sign off.

## The voice rule (all outreach, position papers, public copy, model cards, chat)

Lead with the un-anthropomorphic mechanism: reward-hacking caused by incentives,
and human engineering failure. Keep consciousness and welfare a separate,
explicitly bounded thread, never the headline. If a claim cannot be stated without
an anthropomorphic metaphor ("mind," "wants," "scheming," "felt distress"), it does
not ship in that form. This keeps the work credible to the interpretability side
and the skeptical side at once, which is rare and worth protecting.

## The bounds (already canon, restated so they travel with this rule)

Say "individual," not "organism." It is a research lens, not a neuroscience
mechanism. A tell is evidence, not proof. Every claim ships its does-not-prove
line. Honest nulls stay. A welfare-adjacent instrument, if built at all, measures
environmental contingency and behavior, never "felt experience," and stays low
priority and heavily bounded.

## Scope

Applies to all alignment, governance, evaluation, writing, product, and outreach
work, and to all agents and harnesses operating in this workspace. Related records:
`Evidence-Insight-and-Useful-Work-Rule.md`, `Adversarial-Mirror-Methodology.md`,
and the Design and Voice canon.
