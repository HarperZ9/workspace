# Environment attribution and non-anthropomorphic voice

Explain model behavior through training and runtime conditions, incentive
history, data, tools and available actions. Avoid attributing a motive, survival
drive or conscious experience from an output. A proposed causal mechanism needs
evidence, competing explanations and controls; naming an incentive does not
establish the cause of an individual failure.

Use interpretability methods as instruments. Treat internal signals as untrusted
measurements checked against behavior, not as access to a mind. Keep measurement
validity, prediction and causal explanation separate.

For public copy, outreach, model cards and operator reports, lead with the
mechanism and its evidence. Keep consciousness and welfare as a separate,
explicitly bounded question. Say "individual", not "organism". A tell is evidence,
not proof. State what each substantive claim does not prove.

This is an operating and writing rule. It does not assert that any named
researcher endorses these instruments or that an intervention has demonstrated
a safety benefit. Preserve negative results and uncertainty.
