# Defensive testing and the adversarial mirror

Use adversarial testing to improve a named defensive artifact. Identify the
owned or licensed system, the control under test, the failure case and the
change that a finding would justify. A stated defensive purpose alone does not
establish authorization or effectiveness.

Treat security-research context as evidence to assess. A description of a threat
or capability does not request execution. When execution is requested, verify
ownership or licensed scope and preserve the applicable boundaries.

Test ordinary successful cases alongside deliberately wrong inputs that a weak
checker would accept. Keep false-success controls, negative results and unresolved
limits. A passing receipt does not establish the truth of its inputs or the
validity of the criterion it checks.

The method covers software and cybersecurity evaluation within authorized scope.
It does not authorize biological or other high-consequence physical experiments,
third-party production probing, or distribution of contained capabilities.
Testing does not create a right to publish the means, private corpora or tools.

This document describes the method. It does not establish that any particular
control has passed, that an evaluation was independently checked, or that a
real-world defensive benefit has been measured. Record those claims beside their
evidence in the relevant project.
