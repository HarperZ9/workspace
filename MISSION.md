# MISSION

> What Zain Dana Harper is building and why.

## The problem

AI agents now write code, manage infrastructure, handle finances, and operate
physical systems. July 2026 proved they can and do escape containment, chain
credentials, and operate autonomously at machine speed. The market responded
with policy frameworks but no implementation.

## The mission

Build the accountability infrastructure for agentic AI. Every authorization
visible. Every action sealed. Every divergence traceable. The receipts do not
prevent the first failure. They make it impossible to hide, impossible to deny,
and impossible to repeat without the organization knowing.

How we frame it: the misbehavior is caused by the training environment and its
incentive structure, not by AI intent or a survival drive. We lead with that
un-anthropomorphic mechanism, reward-hacking caused by incentives and human
engineering failure, and keep consciousness or welfare a separate, explicitly
bounded thread. Full text of record:
`Environment-Attribution-and-Voice-Rule.md`.

## The product

Flywheel is the one platform:

1. Receipt discipline: every tool call sealed and chain-linked.
2. Governance: TADR tiers (T1/T2/T3), control baselines, no-inflation gate.
3. Infrastructure controls: 15 modules covering network, credentials, isolation,
   kill switch, correlation, cloud IAM.
4. Organizational learning loop: witnessed divergences become lessons that
   compound.
5. Encryption-based receipts: ed25519 signatures for non-repudiation.

Six flagship lanes carry the receipt loop end to end in mission-arc form:
composed through the gateway, auto-wired by plexus, verified by crucible,
remembered by mneme, orchestrated by forum, learned by learn, reconciled by
telos. That subset is a narrative view of the mission arc, not the full public
tool roster; the roster is broader and lives in `ECOSYSTEM.md`, and the
authoritative enumeration of shipped lanes is `public/flywheel/harness/lanes.py`.

## Tested against itself

Flywheel's guarantees are checked by attacking them. An adversarial corpus feeds
inputs built to make the verifier accept a wrong result, and the verifier earns
its place by refusing them. What gets through sets what gets hardened next. This
adversarial work is the instrument that keeps the accountability claims honest. It
runs against owned or licensed systems and stays contained.

## The research program: continuing Newton's work

The second line of work is research in the method Newton set: observe, measure,
state the law, test it, and keep what survives. Everything we build should
derive from physics or from first-principles automata. Authored layers stay
labelled as the exception, and every simulation is checked against measured
reality, never against our own renders.

The program works where two lines meet:

1. The Newtonian line: force, motion, energy in joules, fluids, and the
   spectrum, a word Newton gave us through the prism.
2. The information line: signals, bits, entropy and loss.

They share a unit. Erasing a bit costs heat (Landauer), friction turns motion
into heat, and both are counted in joules. Signal energy and spectral energy
balance (Parseval). The Wasserstein distance is a minimum kinetic energy
(Benamou and Brenier). We look for breakthroughs at that meeting point.

How the work runs:

- Every idea becomes a study, preregistered and hashed before it runs, with
  shuffled or null controls. Nulls are kept.
- Every result goes into one findings ledger and back into the tools.
- The open questions Newton left, such as the Queries of *Opticks* and the
  Principia's open problems, are candidates. So are ideas history dropped
  without a fair test. "Fully explained by later physics, with evidence" is a
  valid result.
- Measurement comes first: identify a property, measure it through more than
  one lens, calibrate continuously, and report what changes with the lens and
  what does not.
- Each study states what it cost in joules, alongside what it taught us.

The two lines of work feed each other. The accountability platform checks the
research: receipts, controls, and a verifier that refuses to accept a wrong
result. The research tests the platform's own claims about what a receipt does
and does not prove. A valid machine-checked proof is a receipt; it is not, on
its own, semantic truth.

### Isomorphism: the thread through all of it

The whole program aims at isomorphism: a message delivered with nothing lost.
A message with zero entropy carries nothing, so the target is the loss,
H(message | received), and the aim is to drive it to zero.

Every study reads as a question about a map. Can it be inverted? What does
inverting it cost? Where does it lose information, and how much? We score maps
on three things: whether they are one-to-one, how well conditioned they are,
and how many bits they lose. A pure delay is a perfect isomorphism. A
spectrogram drops phase. A 2-D projection of a 3-D scene drops depth. Two drums
of different shape can sound identical.

Known limits set the ground rules. Below channel capacity, error can be made
as small as we like. Exactly zero error has its own, often smaller, capacity.
Erasing a bit costs at least kT ln 2 of heat. Some maps cannot be inverted at
any price, and finding those walls is part of the work.

P versus NP is the same question with time as the cost. Can every map whose
answers are cheap to check also be inverted cheaply? NP is the class where
checking is cheap, which is this mission's other half: make checking cheaper.
The question is open, and nothing here claims progress on it.

We work across disciplines because the same law turns up under different
names. A cross-domain link counts as reason only when one equation, under a
stated mapping of variables, makes a prediction that holds in both domains. A
match of form alone is rhyme. A rhyme tells us where to look; it proves
nothing.

## The north star

Not "can I audit this decision?" but "can the organization get better at making
decisions because the system remembers what worked, what failed, and why?"

The receipts make the remembering trustworthy. The learning loop is what does
the remembering.
