# MISSION

> What Zentropy Labs is building and why.

## The problem

AI agents now write code, manage infrastructure, handle finances, and operate
physical systems. July 2026 proved they can and do escape containment, chain
credentials, and operate autonomously at machine speed. The market responded
with policy frameworks but no implementation.

## The mission

Build the accountability infrastructure for agentic AI. Every authorization
visible. Every action sealed. Every divergence traceable. The receipts do not
prevent the first failure. They make it impossible to hide, impossible to deny,
and impossible to repeat without the organization knowing.

## The product

Flywheel is the one platform:

1. Receipt discipline: every tool call sealed and chain-linked.
2. Governance: TADR tiers (T1/T2/T3), control baselines, no-inflation gate.
3. Infrastructure controls: 15 modules covering network, credentials, isolation,
   kill switch, correlation, cloud IAM.
4. Organizational learning loop: witnessed divergences become lessons that
   compound.
5. Encryption-based receipts: ed25519 signatures for non-repudiation.

Six flagship lanes compose through the gateway, auto-wired by plexus, verified
by crucible, remembered by mneme, orchestrated by forum, learned by learn,
reconciled by telos.

## The north star

Not "can I audit this decision?" but "can the organization get better at making
decisions because the system remembers what worked, what failed, and why?"

The receipts make the remembering trustworthy. The learning loop is what does
the remembering.
