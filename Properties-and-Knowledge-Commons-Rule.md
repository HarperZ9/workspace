# Properties over property: knowledge, balance and synthesis

Rule of record, set by the author on 2026-11-02. This file is the full text, and index entry 13 in `C:/dev/CLAUDE.md` and `C:/dev/AGENTS.md` is the short form. The author's words are quoted verbatim with their date.

## 1. Scope of the research
"The study of mechanisms, interactions, relationships, and patterns across nature, technology, humanity, and the hypothetical/potential", extended the same day: "Digital and physical space, state, and all others."

Isomorphism is the method that joins these domains (the mission, 2026-10-29). A pattern found in one domain is written as a map that can fail, then tested in another. Every study names the domains, spaces and states it bridges.

## 2. Knowledge has no owner
"Owner rights, but on a deeper level; no owner." Asked what has no owner, the author answered: "knowledge". In another session the same day: "I believe in free and open knowledge and mostly free markets."

- People may own hardware, files and services. How a thing works is knowledge, and walling it off or hiding it withholds knowledge from the people who live with it.
- Methods, data, results and negative results are published openly, under the licence split already in force (text CC BY, code MIT or FSL).
- Reading weights to learn what a model learned and what shaped it is recovering knowledge.
- Limit: open knowledge does not mean publishing working harm. Live exploit tooling, methods that locate or expose people, and self-editing tooling for frontier-scale models stay contained under the delayed-disclosure rule. The mechanism and the lesson are published; the tool is not.

## 3. Properties over property
"properties over property ~ functional balance, and always measuring and calibrating to feedback with elasticity and plasticity as driving goals."

- Describe and judge systems by their measured properties: what they do and what they are, not who holds them or what they are called.
- Every system has a feedback loop: measure, calibrate, measure again.
- Elasticity is recovering form after a disturbance. Plasticity is lasting, useful change under sustained pressure. The aim is a functional balance of the two. A system that is only elastic never learns, and one that is only plastic holds no shape. A system with neither is brittle: it breaks without bending.
- Where it applies, a study reports both: recovery after a perturbation, and lasting change per unit of feedback.

## 4. Synthesis across every predecessor
"our synthesis of combining the techniques of all of our predecessors across all pools of knowledge and speciality."

- Before building, gather what predecessors in every relevant field already found, including fields that use other names for the same idea. The prior-art search comes before any claim that something is absent.
- Credit the sources and transform what we take. This extends rule 12, the synthesis and design framework, and the 2026-10-30 ruling on taking ideas by transformation.
- Combinations are the unit of new work: two or more predecessors' techniques joined, with one first test that could fail.

## What this rule does not change
It overrides no authorization, privacy, consent, licence, third-party rights, accessibility, disclosure or release requirement.
