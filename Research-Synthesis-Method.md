# Research synthesis method

Adopted by the operator on 2026-10-02 for all research in this workspace and for the agent's own work. Source: Lilian Weng's account of how she writes Lil'Log posts (lilianweng.github.io), as summarized in a 2026-10-01 X post. Her seven steps form the spine. The additions in each step come from the workspace's rules of record and keep the method honest about what is shown versus inferred.

## The seven steps

1. **Anchor on one clear research direction.** Name the theme in one sentence before reading. Workspace addition: name the decision or build the synthesis should inform, per the evaluation-to-decision rule. If no decision would change, ask whether the work is worth doing.

2. **Read widely around the theme.** Cover the key results, including the ones that cut against the expected story. Workspace addition: prefer primary sources; record what could not be read (paywalls, 403s) as a coverage gap, never fill it from memory. Record the date each source was read.

3. **Organize everything read into a clear classification.** Build a taxonomy before writing prose. Workspace addition: a source may sit in several categories; the taxonomy is a working tool and gets revised when a source does not fit.

4. **Extract the shared logic, core ideas or unified mathematical framework.** Find the thread that runs through all the work. Workspace addition: state the framework as mechanisms or equations that make predictions, so it can be tested. Note which sources fit it badly; those are where new results hide.

5. **Step outside individual papers and reorganize around the distilled storyline.** Write around the framework, not paper by paper. Workspace addition: the storyline must still let a reader trace each claim back to its source.

6. **Use your own understanding and derivations to fill gaps between studies.** Workspace addition, binding: every gap-filling claim is labeled inferred or proposed, kept separate from what sources show, and given its cheapest test. A derivation is a hypothesis until checked. This is the step where a synthesis most easily overclaims.

7. **Structure the result as a logically smooth, progressively layered argument.** A reader should follow it start to finish. Workspace addition: lead with the result or decision; put the essential limit beside each claim; keep honest nulls and dropped ideas in their own section; write in the operator's prose rules.

## Applied to this workspace

- **Research notes and reports** (mission-control, essays, model cards, outreach): follow the seven steps; the report states the anchor and decision at the top and has a coverage-gaps section.
- **Workflows and agent fan-outs:** map phases to the steps. Inventory (2), classify (3), extract framework (4), synthesize around it (5), derive and test gaps (6, with adversarial vetting), write (7). Pass sources between phases by file path.
- **The agent's own work:** before writing any synthesis or recommendation, run the steps in miniature: one-sentence anchor and decision, sources actually read, a grouping, the shared idea, and every inference marked. When a reply fills a gap from reasoning, say so in the reply.
- **Shared material from the operator:** treat each item as input to step 2 for the current anchors, judged objectively: what is new on its merits, what it enables us to build or advance, where it is weak, and how it connects to other items.

## Relation to other rules of record

Evidence, insight and useful work governs claims and effort; this file governs how a synthesis is built. Environment attribution governs how AI behavior is explained inside a synthesis. Just culture governs how mistakes in a synthesis are reported and corrected.
